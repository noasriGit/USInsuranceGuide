If you garage a registered vehicle in Bethesda, Maryland, you must meet Maryland auto insurance rules: 30/60/15 liability, matching UM/UIM, and a PIP offer you may waive in writing. Bethesda is a close-in Montgomery County community, not a separate insurance jurisdiction. Wisconsin Avenue, NIH, Walter Reed, and Beltway commuting change how the car is parked and how many miles it runs. They do not rewrite the statute.

This page does not invent Bethesda premiums or claim that close-in property values produce a published local rate. For the legal floor, use the [Maryland auto insurance overview](/states/maryland/auto-insurance/) and the [Maryland auto insurance requirements guide](/states/maryland/auto-insurance/requirements/). For statewide cost context, use the [Maryland auto insurance cost page](/states/maryland/auto-insurance/cost/).

## Closer-in than Rockville, denser than farther-out suburbs

Bethesda sits inside the Beltway on the Wisconsin Avenue corridor, with large employment sites at NIH and Walter Reed National Military Medical Center and a mixed-use downtown. Housing and parking are denser than in many farther-out Montgomery County suburbs: high-rises, condo buildings, and garage-adjacent street grids rather than only single-family driveways.

That is the local contrast with [Rockville auto insurance](/states/maryland/rockville/). Rockville is the county seat farther up I-270, with a broader mix of driveways and apartment lots. Bethesda trips more often start in a structured garage, on a meter, or on a residential side street, then move onto Wisconsin Avenue or I-495. Same Maryland Insurance Administration. Different answers on garaging type, commute, and whether the car sits all day near a campus garage.

[Montgomery County's website](https://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/) is the local government source for county services. It does not publish a Bethesda-only liability limit. The [Maryland Insurance Administration](https://insurance.maryland.gov/) and [MVA insurance requirements](https://mva.maryland.gov/title-registration/auto-insurance-requirements) do.

Nearby [Silver Spring renters insurance](/states/maryland/silver-spring/) describes a more renter-heavy downtown. Many Bethesda households own or rent in condo-adjacent buildings and still keep a car. Renters or condo policies do not satisfy MVA.

## Maryland rules that apply in Bethesda

| Coverage | Minimum limit |
|---|---|
| Bodily injury, one person | $30,000 |
| Bodily injury, per accident | $60,000 |
| Property damage, per accident | $15,000 |

UM/UIM minimums match those figures. Insurers must offer at least $2,500 in PIP unless you complete an affirmative written waiver. Limited PIP is another structured choice. Parking in a Bethesda garage does not waive PIP for you.

Maryland remains generally at-fault for bodily injury liability. PIP can pay certain medical bills and lost wages without a fault finding, up to the limit. Read the waiver form. Ask what health insurance does and does not replace.

If you work in the District, [Washington, D.C. auto insurance](/states/washington-dc/auto-insurance/) uses different minimums. If you work in Virginia, [Virginia auto insurance requirements](/states/virginia/auto-insurance/requirements/) now use 50/100/25 for current policies. Neither substitution works for a Maryland registration. Keep the Bethesda garaging address on the policy, not the office garage in another jurisdiction, unless that is truly where the car lives.

## Parking patterns that change optional coverages

Close-in mixed-use parking is the practical Bethesda difference:

- Structured garages under high-rises and condos: low-speed ramps, tight stalls, and shared access
- Street parking along commercial and residential blocks near Wisconsin Avenue
- Campus or employer lots at NIH, Walter Reed, or nearby offices
- Beltway park-and-ride patterns for people who drive only the first or last miles

Collision is the coverage that may respond to damage you cause to your own car in a garage or on a street. Comprehensive may respond to theft, vandalism, glass, and certain weather events while the car is parked. Maryland does not require those optional coverages. A loan or lease often does.

Higher surrounding property values and denser mixed-use blocks are a reason to talk about liability limits above the legal floor. They are not a published Bethesda premium and not a reason to invent a rate on this page. Medical bills and vehicle repairs can exceed 30/60/15 after a serious crash anywhere in the county.

Auto comprehensive is still not flood insurance for a condo or rental. Use [FloodSmart](https://www.floodsmart.gov/) for rising-water questions at a residence.

## MVA verification and lapses

MVA checks coverage electronically. A lapse can trigger $150 for the first 30 days without verified insurance and $7 per additional day, plus possible registration suspension and an FR-19. Those figures are statewide. Bethesda does not add a city fee in this guide.

Carry proof of insurance. If you change carriers after a move from Rockville or from the District, ask how the electronic record is updated. A one-day gap can look like a lapse even when you intended a clean switch.

## Coverage combinations that matter in Bethesda

Talk through these combinations with a licensed Maryland producer:

- Liability and UM/UIM at or above 30/60/15, with a frank look at assets in a higher-value close-in market
- Full PIP, limited PIP, or a written waiver
- Collision and comprehensive matched to garage, street, or campus parking
- Rental reimbursement if you depend on the car for Beltway or Wisconsin Avenue trips
- Accurate one-way commute mileage, including days the car stays parked
- Coordinating auto with a condo (HO-6) or renters policy if you live in a mixed-use building

Do not copy a Rockville driveway description onto a Bethesda garage application. Do not copy a D.C. policy onto a Maryland title.
